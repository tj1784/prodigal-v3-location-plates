The same place as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the ground just outside the arch, about a metre from the nearest stone, looking southeast through the opening. Worn trodden earth fills the lower frame and meets the stone paving of the porch, grit and small chips in the joints. The arch's pale limestone piers rise at the left and right edges, one metre ahead in the centre. The morning shade lies on the right, two metres off. The faded cloth with the bronze balance and the pouch of stone weights sits two metres ahead on the RIGHT, and the small clay pot of banked coals rests about four metres ahead, also on the right. The elders' bench is five metres ahead on the RIGHT, and the open timber door is five metres ahead in the centre, its leaf swung back onto the street. Flat-roofed houses stand four metres off on both sides, further roofs beyond them. The low morning sun stands ahead on the left: the arch is backlit, and the shade under it covers the paving and a third and more of the frame in deep shadow. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
