The same place as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, as one close-up texture filling the whole frame: the outer limestone of the arch's southwest pier, the lens under half a metre from the block. Rough pale fieldstone fills the picture, every pit, crack, lichen speck and grain of dust sharp, the joints packed with worn mortar and grit, the edge smoothed where hands and feet pass every day, the arris chipped. The low morning sun stands ahead and rakes that raised edge at a grazing angle, so the broken rim catches a warm line of light and the face toward the lens rests in deep shade, the shadow inside each joint dark and wide. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
