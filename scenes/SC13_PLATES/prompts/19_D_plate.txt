The same farmyard as the reference image, now at night, the stand point in the courtyard by the house, a new fire burning on the open earth. A wide clay basin of warm water sits on the packed ground at the right edge, close to the lens. Four metres ahead in the centre a wood fire burns, and it is the light of this view. The house stands eight metres ahead, fieldstone and mud brick under a roof of timber, reeds and packed earth, its timber doorway in the centre and a torch burning at the eaves above the door. The eaves run to the left at seven metres, and the yard spreads to the left. The dry-stone yard wall stands ten metres to the right. Two trees rise beyond the roof, one in the centre at twenty-nine metres and one to the left at thirty-two, and the terrace edge lies far to the left at forty-three metres. A spit fire burns four metres ahead on the left. Behind the camera the gate lamp burns at twenty-six metres, lamps burn on the road up the hill at thirty-six metres, and eight smaller lamps burn low around the yard. The fire backlights the doorway. Deep shadow fills the eaves, the inner jamb and the ground along the wall, a third of the frame and more. Smoke from the yard fire hangs low in the still air. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
