The same place as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the right: the outside face of the waist-high dry-stone wall runs away from the right-hand pier along the left of the frame, straight down the slope toward the lake, and the old olive grove fills the right, its nearest gnarled trunk three metres off and more trunks and low crowns beyond. Between the trunks and past the end of the wall, the slope falls to the pale lake, thirty metres below, and the far shore's grey hills. The low sun is ahead on the left through dusty haze: it slants through the grove, the trunks' near faces in deep shadow, their long shadows reaching toward the camera and to the right across fallen leaves. Muted sun-faded earth colours, olive-grey leaves, a pale warm-grey sky, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
