## Where the spot stands

Sol's stand point in `orbits/sc16/FIELDS.json`, used here: (-2, -11), facing 60°. The orbit spot's own pos is (4.3, -4.1), facing 118°. Digest at (-2, -11), facing 60°:

- **Plate (60°).** Ledge, lead seal, fieldstone wall and old olive on the LEFT (eleven to twelve metres). Low fire at the centre, twelve metres ahead, the view backlit. Open gate thirty metres on the RIGHT. Wax tablet and loose coping stone thirty-two metres on the RIGHT. Grove trunks from thirty-three metres on the RIGHT. Dark lake forty-two metres on the LEFT. Four of the six clay lamps in frame: near the centre at twelve metres, on the LEFT at fifteen, on the RIGHT at fifteen and twenty-six.
- **Right (330°).** Wall seven metres on the RIGHT. Gate thirty metres on the LEFT, grove from thirty-three metres on the LEFT. Fire to the left, twelve metres, outside the frame. Three lamps: fifteen metres on the LEFT (the plate's far-right lamp), twenty-two metres on the LEFT, sixteen metres near the centre.
- **Reverse (240°).** Wall two metres on the RIGHT, rising terraces beyond. Fire behind the camera, twelve metres, the view lit from the front. The lamps stay in the other quarters.
- **Left (150°).** Wall two metres on the LEFT. Lake forty-two metres on the RIGHT. Fire to the right, twelve metres, outside the frame, and the lamps with it. The olive's centre sits just outside this quarter, so the prompt leaves the crown out.
- **Low corner.** Lens at the uphill end of the ledge, looking downhill. Olive on the LEFT, lake on the RIGHT, fire off to the RIGHT.
- **Macro.** The worn upper lip of the ledge fills the frame. The fire grazes it from the gateward side.

The low fire is the one flame. The six clay lamps keep small flames in their bowls. The screenplay has those lamps in hands; these plates are empty, so the lamps rest on the ground at their staged points.

## Locked design

The Scene 1 hillside, at night. Waist-high dry-stone wall of rough unmortared fieldstone. Eastern opening 3.2 metres between two square piers. One weathered timber leaf, diagonal brace and rope lashings, hinged on the uphill pier and swung outward. Threshold of trodden earth. Natural pale limestone ledge at (0, 0), knee-high, worn hollow, with the torn barley round and crumbs. Ancient olive at (-2.6, 1.4). Low fire at (3.2, -0.4). Lead seal and cord at (0.57, -0.21). Loose coping stone at (27, 2.6) and wax tablet at (27, 2.15), on the wall lakeward of the gate. Six clay lamps at (5.72, -2.38), (2.62, 3.56), (12.14, -6.65), (18.81, 4.23), (20.14, -11.35) and (11.84, -18.65). Exterior olive grove beyond the gate. Dark lake and grey far shore downhill. Breeze off the lake, smoke off the embers, dust off the dry terraces. Empty of people.
