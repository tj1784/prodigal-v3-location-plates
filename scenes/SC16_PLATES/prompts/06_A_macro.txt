The same worn upper lip of the limestone ledge as the reference image fills the whole frame. Chalk-pale bedrock, shallow polished hollows, fine pits and hairline cracks hold settled earth, two small barley crumbs and one fallen olive leaf. The stone is a natural shelf, knee-high in the wide view, its top worn smooth and slightly dished by long use, with grit caught in the grain. The low fire on the gateward side of the ledge is the one flame and the only light, grazing the raised grains with restrained amber while the pits and crevices fall dark. A little ash-fine dust sits in the sheltered cracks, and the edge of the olive leaf curls where it has dried. The amber falls off within the width of the lip, and the turned-away hollows stay in real deep shadow. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
