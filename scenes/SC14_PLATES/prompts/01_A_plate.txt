The shell stays seven paces by four, 5.25 metres north to south and 3 metres across: mud brick, soot-dark beams. Two metres ahead the fire-blackened hearth ring burns at centre around a clay pot. The arched lamp niche is ahead on the LEFT, three metres off, a small clay flame inside the arch. Sealed storage jars and oil jars line the west wall on the LEFT. A clay water jar enters on the RIGHT, two metres off. One untouched round of flat bread lies ahead on the LEFT of the table. A high stack of bread sits under the lens, slightly RIGHT, and a set place waits on the south end behind the lens. The hearth fire ahead is the only light inside, soot-brown. It reaches the pot, the near jars and the untouched round, and the south half falls into deep shadow. The yard fire, the spit and the eaves torch stay off-frame to the RIGHT. The fold lies fourteen metres RIGHT, beyond the east wall. Roof recesses and the near table hold more than a third of the frame dark. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
