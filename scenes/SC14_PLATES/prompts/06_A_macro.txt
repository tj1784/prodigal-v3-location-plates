The same room as the reference image, at night, as a close texture of the low olive-wood table. The lens comes within a hand's breadth of the rubbed top, facing north along the grain, just south of one untouched round of flat bread. Worn olive wood fills the frame: old knife tracks, dark oil sunk in the pores, pale flour caught in the scratches, and the blistered crust of that one round entering ahead, slightly to the LEFT. The high stack of bread sits behind the lens on the RIGHT and stays outside this close frame. A set place lies further behind, also outside the frame. The hearth fire lies ahead at the centre, about two metres off, the only light on this surface, a restrained soot-brown flame grazing the raised grain and the near crust. The niche lamp lies ahead on the LEFT, about three metres off, its small flame staying inside the arch and leaving this wood to the hearth. The yard fire stands off to the RIGHT and the eaves torch ahead on the RIGHT, both outside this close frame. Grooves, flour pockets and the near side of the crust hold more than a third of the surface in deep contact shadow. The flour belongs to the mill and to the bread stacked on this table. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
