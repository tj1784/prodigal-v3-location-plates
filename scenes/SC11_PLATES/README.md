## The locked design

- **The wall:** waist-high, rough unmortared fieldstone, around the terraced enclosure.
- **The gate:** a 3.2 m opening between two square fieldstone piers. One weathered timber leaf, rope-lashed, with a diagonal brace, hinges on the uphill pier and swings outward. The threshold is scuffed earth.
- **Inside:** the limestone ledge, 2.3 m by 1.4 m, knee-high natural bedrock, 27 m from the gate. The ancient olive stands 2.6 m farther along the hillside and 1.4 m downhill, crown about 7 m across. A torn barley round and its crumbs lie on the ledge.
- **Outside:** the old olive grove.
- **Dusk objects, same positions in every view:** small ground fire at (3.2, −0.4); six fired-clay lamps on the earth at (5.72, −2.38), (2.62, 3.56), (12.14, −6.65), (18.81, 4.23), (20.14, −11.35) and (11.84, −18.65); loose fieldstone on the downhill wall crown at (27, 2.6); wax tablet on the earth at (28.25, 1.95); short tether rope at the inner wall about 3 m uphill of the gateway; one donkey tethered there at (25, −2.8).
- **Beyond:** the pewter lake and the grey far shore.
- **Empty of people.** The hillside keeps the tethered donkey at this hour.
- **Air:** the breeze off the lake moves the olive leaves, bends the fire's smoke and lifts dust off the dry terrace lips. Sparks rise off the fire. Each particle has that cause.
