The same gate and street as the reference image, at the same night, with the lens a hand's breadth above the street paving beside the outside north jamb, looking east along the worn slab. The dark stone corner and the empty clay bowl fill the foreground on the LEFT, rough paving and settled grit under the lens, the jamb and the timber lintel rising above. Through the opening the threshold takes a narrow patch of amber, and the blazing brazier shows about thirty metres ahead in the centre, the crowned stone figure beyond it. The court's flame is ahead and the view is backlit. The corner itself stays in deep shade across a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
