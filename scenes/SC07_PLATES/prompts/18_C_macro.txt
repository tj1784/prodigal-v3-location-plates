The same bare plain as the reference image, at the same evening, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: a wind-worn limestone flake beside the near thorn bush on the north rise, its chalky edge rubbed smooth, fine ochre dust packed into a hairline crack, and a brittle thorn tip resting against the stone. Dry thorn litter and a second pale chip lie in the crease around it, at the foot of the near bush, wind-combed dust on the sheltered side. The low sun, behind the rise on the west, grazes the flake: the rubbed edge turns pale, the face turned from the light stays dark, and the crack holds a line of deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
