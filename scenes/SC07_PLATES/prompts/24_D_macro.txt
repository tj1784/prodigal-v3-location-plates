The same court as the reference image, at the same night, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the north end of the low olive-wood table, rubbed pale grain, shallow old knife marks, a dark wine spill worked into the wood, a few silver coins, and the edge of the wax tablet with the stylus lying along it. Old oil has darkened the grain beside the spill, and a crushed garland leaf sticks at the tablet's corner. The brazier's flame, fifteen metres east on the court axis, grazes the surface from the east: the far rim takes a thin amber line, and the near edge toward the lens falls into deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
