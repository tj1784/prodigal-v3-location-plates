The same bare plain as the reference image, at the same evening and from the same rise, with the camera turned a quarter to the right to face two hundred and forty degrees. The western stretch of the road lies about twenty-seven metres away on the LEFT, running back across the ochre plain, and the thorn shrub about thirty-five metres away lies on the LEFT as well. The near stony rise descends through shadowed folds, wind-set dust in the lee of the stones. The low sun is ahead on the RIGHT, backlighting the western road and sending long shadows toward the camera and to the left. Deep shade holds a third of the folds. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
