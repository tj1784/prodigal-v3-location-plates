The same gate and street as the reference image, at the same night, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the limestone slab beneath the empty bowl at the beggar's corner, a shallow rubbed hollow, fine chips along its edge, old clay abrasion, and settled grit in a hairline crack, with the bowl's dusty fired-clay rim at the edge of the frame. Street dust films the hollow, and grit packs the hairline crack under the bowl's foot. Faint amber from the open gate, the court's brazier beyond the jamb, grazes the outer lip of the stone, and the near side of the hollow, shielded by the north jamb, stays in deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
