The same gate and street as the reference image, at the same night and from the same stand, with the camera turned a quarter to the right to face two hundred and seventy degrees, south along the street. The court's outside west wall stands about six metres away on the LEFT, and the houses across the street, worn lime plaster over limestone, stand about two metres away on the RIGHT. Uneven paving and settled grit lead between them into the dark distance. The service door lies about thirteen metres away on the LEFT, behind the court wall. The brazier and the gateway's flame light come from the LEFT and rake the southward street. The colonnade lamps are ahead on the LEFT, their light held inside the gateway. A narrow amber patch crosses the near paving. The street walls stay deeply dark across a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
