The same court as the reference image, at the same night and from the same paving, with the camera turned a quarter to the right to face two hundred and seventy degrees, south. The south colonnade stands ahead, the outer court wall about ten metres away in the centre, couches and low tables with cups under the tiled walk. The smaller service door sits in the south wall near its west end, about thirteen metres away on the RIGHT, and the street beyond shows about fifteen metres away on the RIGHT. Open paving fills the foreground and runs on past the left edge toward the main table. The brazier light comes from the LEFT and throws long shadows to the right. The southern hanging lamp is ahead on the LEFT, its own small pool on the walk, and the roof between the lamps stays deeply dark across a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
