The same road crest as the reference image, at the same sunrise, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: a crossing stone in the three-metre crest road, its pale limestone crown polished smooth by wheels, shallow pits holding ochre dust, a fine runoff crack, and a dry root fibre caught at the edge. Packed earth and a pressed pebble sit in the groove against the stone's flank, with a second chip wedged in the crack. The low sun from five degrees, straight down the road, grazes the crown from the east: the polished top takes a thin bright line, the west face toward the lens stays dark, and the pit bottoms hold deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
