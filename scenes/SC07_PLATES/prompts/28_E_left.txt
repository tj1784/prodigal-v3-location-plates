The same court at grey dawn as the reference image, from the same stand, with the camera turned a quarter to the left to face ninety degrees, north. The north colonnade stands ahead, the columns about eight metres away on the RIGHT, the outer wall about ten metres ahead in the centre, and the reed mat with flutes and drum about four metres away on the RIGHT. The street opens about fifteen metres away on the LEFT. The dawn is to the RIGHT and rakes the colonnade, long shadows falling left. The ash trail travels west, which in this turn is toward the left. Spent wicks hang in the bays, and the roof holds a third of the frame in deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
