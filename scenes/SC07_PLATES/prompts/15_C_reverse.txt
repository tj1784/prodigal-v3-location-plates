The same bare plain as the reference image, at the same evening and from the same rise, with the camera turned half round to face one hundred and fifty degrees. Open upper ground of the low rise runs away to the north-west, pale limestone flakes and shallow earth folds from the foreground to a bare hazy horizon. The rise climbs gently, and the road lies behind the camera. Wind has combed fine dust onto the sheltered sides of the stones. The low sun is ahead on the LEFT, raking the upper rise and sending long shadows toward the camera and to the right. The gullied folds hold a third of the frame in deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
