The same Greek peristyle court as the reference image, the same twenty-six columns, open shrine, crowned stone figure of Tyche, couches, low table, reed mat, gate and plastered walls, now at the grey of dawn. The camera has moved five centimetres east and twenty-five centimetres north of the night stand, and still faces due east. The reed mat with flutes and drum lies about four metres away on the LEFT. The main couch is about nine metres ahead, the low table about ten metres ahead in the centre with its cups, tablet, stylus, purse, silver and wine spill still set, and the court wall recedes about fourteen metres away on the LEFT. The brazier stands fifteen metres ahead in the centre, holding loose pale ash, and the crowned stone figure stands about seventeen metres ahead in the centre inside the shrine. One grey dawn comes from five degrees, straight ahead over the shrine wall, and the view is backlit: couch and table throw long shadows west toward the camera, and the roofed walks stay in deep shadow across a third of the frame. A hot wind out of the east lifts the ash and carries a thin trail west over the couches. The hanging lamps hold dark spent wicks. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
