The same gate and street as the reference image, at the same night and from the same stand, with the camera turned a quarter to the left to face ninety degrees, north along the street. The opposite house fronts stand about two metres away on the LEFT, and the court's outside wall stands about six metres away on the RIGHT, grit gathered at its dark foot. Uneven paving leads away between the walls. The gateway and the bowl on its outside corner lie just past the right edge of this turn, back in the eastward view. The brazier and the gateway light are to the RIGHT. The colonnade lamps are behind on the RIGHT. Beyond the small threshold pool the northward street stays deeply dark across a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
