The same road crest as the reference image, at the same sunrise and from the same stand on the south shoulder, with the camera turned a quarter to the right to face two hundred and seventy-four degrees, almost due south. Open stony ground fills the frame. The bare south shoulder falls from the ridge into a shallow fold, dry limestone shelves and sparse grass running from two metres out to the farther slope. The crest continues off the left edge, and the misted valley falls away farther east on that side. This quarter turn sees open ground. The low sunrise stands to the LEFT, raking the shoulder and throwing the stones' long shadows to the right. The fold's own shade holds a third of the near ground. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
