The same gate and street as the reference image, at the same night and from the same stand, with the camera turned half round to face one hundred and eighty degrees, west toward the houses across the street. The opposite house front stands about one metre ahead, its plastered face on the LEFT, and the street's near paving lies about one metre ahead on the RIGHT, scuffed stone continuing along both sides. The gate is behind the camera. Its amber reaches this lower wall from behind the lens, so the view is lit from the front, and the far sides of the wall remain in deep shade across a third of the frame. Smoke from the court drifts above the roofline behind the camera. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
