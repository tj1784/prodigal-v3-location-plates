The same court as the reference image, at the same night and from the same paving, with the camera turned a quarter to the left to face ninety degrees, north. The north colonnade stands ahead, the run of twenty-six columns showing about eight metres away on the RIGHT, the outer wall about ten metres ahead in the centre. Couches and small tables sit under the dark timber roof, lamps burning in the bays. The reed mat with its flutes and drum lies about four metres away on the RIGHT. The street opens about fifteen metres away on the LEFT. The brazier is to the RIGHT and its long shadows fall left. The southern lamp is behind on the RIGHT. Deep shade holds the roof and the gaps between the lamp pools across a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
