The same Jordan ford as the reference image, at the same noon and from the same stand, with the camera turned a quarter to the left to face one hundred degrees, upstream to the north. The west approach lies about four metres away on the RIGHT, the brown water about five metres away on the RIGHT beyond it, and the dry west bank on the LEFT. The northern west-bank tamarisk stands nine metres ahead near the centre, exposed roots reaching down toward the water on the right, fallen leaves under its own branches. The high sun is behind the camera, so the upstream bank is lit from the front, and short shadows extend away to the north. The dark undercut bank holds a third of the frame in deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
