The same Jordan ford as the reference image, at the same noon, with the lens a hand's breadth above dry gravel at the west-bank lip, south of the ford entry, looking east across the brown shallows. Rounded pebbles, damp silt and a dark exposed root fill the foreground. The ford entry lies on the LEFT, the quick brown water on the RIGHT, the east bank and its two tamarisks beyond, one on the RIGHT and one near the centre. The high sun is to the RIGHT. The southern thicket's dense shade covers the near margin across a third of the frame, and heat shimmers over the far gravel. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
