The same court at grey dawn as the reference image, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the same north end of the low olive-wood table, rubbed pale grain, shallow old knife marks, the dark wine spill, a few silver coins, and the edge of the wax tablet with the stylus along it. The spill and the coins sit where the night left them. A film of pale ash, carried west on the dawn wind, dusts the near coins and the spill. Grey dawn from five degrees, over the shrine wall to the east, grazes the wood: the far rim turns pale, and the near side toward the lens falls into deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
