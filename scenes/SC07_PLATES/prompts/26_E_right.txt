The same court at grey dawn as the reference image, from the same stand, with the camera turned a quarter to the right to face two hundred and seventy degrees, south. The south colonnade and its couches stand ahead, the outer wall about eleven metres away in the centre, the service door about thirteen metres away on the RIGHT, and the street about fifteen metres away on the RIGHT. The dawn is to the LEFT and sends the column and couch shadows to the right. The ash trail from the brazier crosses toward the right, still travelling west on the hot east wind. The roofed walk keeps a third of the frame in deep shade, and the lamp bowls along it hold spent wicks. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
