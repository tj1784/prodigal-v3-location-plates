The same bare plain as the reference image, at the same evening, with the lens a hand's breadth above a stony crease beside the thorn bush on the north rise, looking down the slope. Ochre dust, a pale limestone flake and dry thorn roots fill the foreground, the bush itself on the RIGHT. The road lies about twenty-seven metres away on the RIGHT, the three far thorn shrubs on the RIGHT beyond it, the dust whirl a little to the LEFT of centre, and the low city ahead on the LEFT, its first lamp points tiny at three kilometres. The evening sun is behind on the RIGHT, and long shadows run toward the city across a third of the frame in deep shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
