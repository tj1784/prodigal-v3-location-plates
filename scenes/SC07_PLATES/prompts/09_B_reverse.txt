The same Jordan ford as the reference image, at the same noon and from the same stand, with the camera turned half round to face one hundred and ninety degrees, west across the dry approach, the river behind the camera. Compressed earth and rounded river gravel fill the foreground, old flood lines cut into the bank. The west bank ground spreads about four metres ahead on the RIGHT. Fine dry plates of earth lie above the damp lower stones. The high sun is to the LEFT, lighting the approach from that side and throwing short shadows to the right. The shaded near ground holds a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
