The same court as the reference image, at the same night, with the lens a hand's breadth above the paving beside the foot of the main couch, looking east past the low table legs. Worn joints and a few crushed garland leaves fill the foreground. The musicians' mat lies on the LEFT. The blazing brazier stands fifteen metres ahead in the centre, the crowned stone figure of Tyche beyond it inside the shrine, and the table with its cups, silver and wine spill sits between. The brazier backlights the legs, long shadows run west toward the lens, and the roofed walks stay deeply dark across a third of the frame. Smoke rises off the coals. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
