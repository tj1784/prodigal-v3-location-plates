The same Jordan ford as the reference image, at the same noon, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: a water-rounded limestone pebble at the west entry of the ford, its upper face dry and dusty, its lower edge dark with river damp, fine brown silt lodged in the shallow pits, and a thin root pressed against the stone. Smaller gravel and a crust of silt surround it where the west entry meets the brown water, a watermark staining the lower third. The high sun from the south catches the dry crown and leaves the damp underside in deep shade, the shadow short and hard on the north side of the pebble. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
