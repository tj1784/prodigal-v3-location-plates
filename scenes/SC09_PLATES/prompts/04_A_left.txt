The same court as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the left, now facing north along the west side of the court. The open paving lies three metres off on the LEFT, the court wall seven metres off on the LEFT, the street seven metres off on the LEFT, and the house fronts fourteen metres off on the LEFT. The right of the frame is the same open paving running north, its stones worn hollow. The thin new moon is to the left. The brazier's flame comes from the right, twenty-one metres off, and the colonnade lamps burn behind on the right, so the west wall takes a side light and its base falls into deep shadow along the left of the frame, more than a third of the picture. A haze of smoke from the lamps softens the far plaster. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
