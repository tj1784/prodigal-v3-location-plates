The same famine market as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the frame: the rim of a bare grain bin, dry split timber, grit packed in the grain, a pale scrape along the near edge, and a fly resting on the dust. The afternoon sun is ahead on the right, grazing the raised grain so each crack holds a real shadow. The same hot east wind has packed the grit into the split. The fly rests there because the bin is bare. The street and the shuttered stalls lie outside this crop. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
