The same famine market as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, the lens a hand's breadth above the street, moved to the paving just north of the grain stall and aimed due south at the bins. The bare timber bins fill the near ground, one metre ahead at the centre, dry and split, flies on the rims, grit caught in the joints. The market street lies one metre off on the RIGHT, grit driving west across it. The shuttered stall face stands one metre off on the LEFT. The afternoon sun is ahead on the right, raking the bin rims and throwing their shadows back toward the lens, and the dark inside the stall on the left covers a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
