The same Kallias's court as the reference image, now on this later night under a thin new moon low in the west. The camera stands on the open paving ten metres west of the court centre, facing due east along the axis. A reed musicians' mat, with flutes and a skin drum, lies nine metres off on the LEFT. The twenty-six limestone columns recede on the LEFT at twelve metres under the clay-tile roof, and the court wall closes that side at fourteen metres. Clay lamps along the colonnade burn fourteen metres off on the RIGHT. The long couch stands cleared fifteen metres ahead at the centre, its wool cushion smooth. The low olive-wood table is sixteen metres ahead at the centre, cups, a wax tablet and a stylus on it, a creased couch on the LEFT of the table and one on the RIGHT. The bronze brazier blazes twenty-one metres ahead at the centre, the open shrine of Tyche twenty-two metres ahead, and the crowned effigy on its plinth twenty-four metres ahead. The moon is behind the camera, over the west gate. The one flame is the brazier ahead: it backlights the table and throws long shadows toward the lens, and the roofed walks hold deep shadow over a third of the frame. Smoke from the brazier hangs in the night air. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
