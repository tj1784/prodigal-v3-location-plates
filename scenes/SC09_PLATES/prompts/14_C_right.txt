The same famine market as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the right, now facing a little west of south, into the near row of stalls. The market street lies three metres off at the centre, and the shuttered stalls fill the centre three metres off, their timber faces close. A stall mouth sits three metres ahead at the centre, another four metres off on the RIGHT, and another four metres off on the LEFT. The hot east wind still drives grit west, across this view from left toward right. The afternoon sun is ahead on the right, raking the shutter boards and striking the grit, so each stall mouth falls into deep shadow and that dark covers more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
