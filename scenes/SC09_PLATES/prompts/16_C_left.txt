The same famine market as the reference image, at the same hour and in the same light, the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the left, now facing a little east of north, into the other stall row. The market street lies three metres off at the centre, and the shuttered stalls fill the centre three metres off, pale timber, iron hooks, boards pulled to. A stall mouth sits three metres ahead at the centre, and another four metres off on the LEFT. Grit from the east wind crosses the lower frame. The afternoon sun is behind on the left, so the stall fronts are partly lit from behind and their mouths hold deep shadow over a third of the frame, the haze thickening the distance. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
