The same gate as the reference image, a quarter turn to the left, facing east-southeast down the cold road. The road east, down to the village gate, lies one metre on the RIGHT and runs away under the moon. The floor track lies six metres ahead at the CENTRE, and the hill track to the pasture seven metres at the CENTRE. The footpath from the village joins thirty-six metres on the RIGHT. The low moon is ahead, and the view is backlit: the road stones are dark against a pale warm-grey haze, and long shadows run toward the camera. The gate lamp, the yard fire, the spit and the eaves torch are all behind the camera, so a warm pool lights the near ground from behind and dies within about four metres, and the road beyond that pool is cold moonlight and deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. The gate itself stays behind the lens. Fine dust lies on the packed road, and the air down the valley is still. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
