The same room as the reference image, a quarter turn to the left, facing west to the jar wall. The oil jars stand two metres on the RIGHT. The stone hand mill on its reed mat, flour spilled across the reeds, lies two metres on the LEFT. The sealed storage jars continue along that wall, just outside the left of the hearth view. The sleeping bench is far to the left behind the mill, outside the bright part of this turn. The hearth is off the frame on the RIGHT, the only light inside, a high soot-brown fire whose glow reaches the near shoulders of the oil jars and leaves the mill and the west wall in deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. The niche lamp sits just outside on the RIGHT, its small flame kept in the arch. The eaves torch is behind the camera, past the east wall, and throws a faint warm edge on the near plaster. Flour dust hangs in that thin light, shaken off the mill. The same mud brick, the same soot-dark beams, the same seven-by-four-pace room. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
