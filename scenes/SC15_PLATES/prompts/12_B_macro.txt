The same room as the reference image, a close texture of the low olive-wood table on the feast night. The lens faces south along the grain, just north of the set place. Worn olive wood fills the frame: old knife tracks, dark oil sunk in the pores, pale flour caught in the scratches. The set place enters at the CENTRE, a clay bowl and one round of flat bread, the crust blistered, a few crumbs in the grain. The stools sit a metre further at the CENTRE, soft beyond the wood. The hand mill lies two metres on the RIGHT and Asher's mat two metres on the LEFT, both outside this close frame. The sleeping bench and the sling peg stay further off. The hearth is behind the camera, about two metres north, and it is the light on this surface, a soot-brown graze across the raised grain and the near crust. The niche lamp is behind the lens as well, its small flame kept in the arch. The eaves torch is behind on the LEFT, outside the frame. Grooves, flour pockets and the near side of the bowl hold more than a third of the surface in deep contact shadow. The flour belongs to the mill and to the bread on this table. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
