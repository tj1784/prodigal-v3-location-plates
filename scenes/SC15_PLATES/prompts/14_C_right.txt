The same yard as the reference image, a quarter turn to the right, facing east-northeast along the house wall. The eaves lie one metre on the LEFT. The house stands three metres on the LEFT, fieldstone and mud brick, and the packed yard runs three metres on the LEFT. The dry-stone yard wall, about 1.7 metres high, lies four metres on the LEFT. The stone fold lies ten metres on the RIGHT, its timber gate barred, the flock bedded inside and the ewe among them. The torch at the eaves is just outside on the LEFT. The hearth and the niche lamp stay inside the house, ahead on the LEFT and outside this turn. The low moon is behind on the RIGHT. The gate lamp lies to the RIGHT, about thirty-two metres off, a small pool at the far gate. The yard fire is behind on the RIGHT, three metres off, and the spit fire is behind the camera, seven metres off, so a warm spill crosses the near earth from behind while the fold and the wall fall into deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. Woodsmoke from the yard fire drifts along the eaves. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
