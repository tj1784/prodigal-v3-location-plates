The same gate as the reference image, a close texture of the stone trough south of the south post. The lens faces southwest, about a metre from the inner lip, and the worn limestone fills the frame: a trough hollowed by long use, the lip polished smooth, hairline cracks packed with dust, a dark water-line, chips along the rim. The packed yard lies two metres on the RIGHT, and the cistern nine metres at the CENTRE, both soft past the stone. The floor track lies twelve metres on the LEFT, the footpath twenty-five metres on the LEFT, the threshing floor twenty-six metres on the LEFT, and the terrace edge twenty-seven metres on the LEFT. The spit fire and the yard fire lie ahead on the RIGHT, about twenty-nine and thirty-one metres off, a warm haze on the far rim. The ovens, the eaves and the house sit further on the RIGHT. The low moon is behind on the LEFT, a cold graze on the near lip. The gate lamp is behind on the RIGHT, outside this close frame, and adds a small warm edge to the right of the rim. The hollow of the trough and the cracks hold more than a third of the stone in deep contact shadow. The dust in the cracks is the dust of the yard. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
