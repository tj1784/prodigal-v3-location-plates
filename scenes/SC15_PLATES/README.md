## The locked design

- **The far field:** ground ploughed today, the last furrow with a wooden plough and an iron share, a heavy wooden yoke, a pair of oxen at the head of the furrow, a path that drops over a dry-stone terrace wall, one terebinth on the headland. Beyond: the road east, the hill track, the hill pasture, other farms, the village, its gate and well, the thorn gully. The flock is up at the fold this night, so the pasture is named as ground.
- **The room:** Eliab's one room, exactly 7 by 4 paces, 5.25 by 3.0 metres. Mud brick, patchy lime plaster, soot-dark beams. Middle of the table (-35.8, -1.8).
- **FRONT, north:** hearth ring, clay pot, arched lamp niche on the left of that wall.
- **RIGHT, east:** open timber doorway, leaf folded in on the north jamb, clay water jar beside it.
- **BEHIND, south:** mud-brick sleeping bench with rolled sheepskins and folded wool blankets; the mat and the sling peg along that wall.
- **LEFT, west:** sealed storage jars and oil jars, stone hand mill on its reed mat with spilled flour.
- **Centre:** low heavy olive-wood table and wooden stools. This hour: bread stacked on the table, a bowl and a round set at the south end. Hearth burning high, soot-brown, glow about 1.7 metres. Niche lamp lit.
- **The yard and the gate:** one gateway in the east yard wall, dry-stone about 1.7 m high. Two posts, a timber lintel, the olive-wood leaf on the south post, folded open into the yard. Clay lamp burning in the arched niche of the north post, pool about 4 m. Stone bench outside, north of the gate. Stone trough south of the south post. Old fig outside to the northeast. Inside: cistern, calf pen, ox byre, barred fold with the flock and the ewe, ovens, the yard fire, the fatted calf on the spit, the torch at the eaves, the house door open.
