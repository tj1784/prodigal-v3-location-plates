The same thorn hollow as the reference image, at the same hard white noon and in the same light, the lens a hand's breadth above the compacted dust inside the sheltered hollow, facing south through the narrow opening. Grit, dry root fibres and small limestone chips fill the lower frame. Low interlocking thorn branches rise close on both sides and cross overhead: a stem three metres away on the RIGHT, further stems five metres away on the LEFT, and a clump six metres ahead near the centre. Small gaps in the branches show the pale sky, and the grey scrub continues about thirty-one metres away at the centre. The same high southern sun is ahead, above the stems, touching the upper tips. Short shadows fall northward, toward the lens. The undersides of the branches and the dust of the hollow stay deeply shaded, covering a third or more of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
