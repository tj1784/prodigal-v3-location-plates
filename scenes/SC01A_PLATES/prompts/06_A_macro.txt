The same foreign farm and field wall as the reference image, at the same hard white noon and in the same light, as one close-up texture filling the whole frame: the worn upper lip of a single pale fieldstone on the wall's southern face. Uneven mineral grains, small pits, a ridge rubbed smooth, a hairline fracture and pale road dust caught in the recesses all stay sharp, every chip and speck legible. The stone is rough fieldstone, chalky and matte, with old settled chips along the edge and a darker seam where two grains meet. The same high southern sun skims the raised grains and the smooth ridge at a grazing angle, so the high points read pale and the low pits fall away. The fracture and the underside of the stone hold deep contact shadow. Fine film grain sits in the dust and in the shadow. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
