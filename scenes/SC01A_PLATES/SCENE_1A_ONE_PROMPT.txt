SCENE 1A: THE FOREIGN FARM ON THE ROAD WEST, AT HARD WHITE NOON. 24 LOCATION PLATES.

Make 24 separate images, one for each numbered shot below, in this order, each 3:2 at the largest size. Every image shows the same place with the same design: the same wall, tamarisk, road, ditch, farm and slope in the same positions. Only the camera position and direction change from shot to shot. Name each image after its shot, for example 01_A_plate.jpg.

THE PLACE (identical in every image)
A first-century AD farm on a road running west through dry, burnt hills. From north to south:
- The farm, uphill in its spring-fed green fold: a low mud-brick farmhouse with a main doorway and a small niche beside it holding a clay household figure and an earthen oil dish; a bare yard with a few quiet hens; a low stone well; a vine over a long weathered table; an open pantry with a heavy timber bar; a clay bread oven with a thin thread of smoke.
- A barley field: the last green barley and pale cut stubble.
- The field wall: waist-high piled fieldstone, nearly a metre thick, running east-west. A 3.5-metre gap opens in it east of the tamarisk, and an earth farm track climbs north through the gap to the farm. The field wall is the road's only wall.
- One single tamarisk with a rough trunk and a low olive-grey crown, growing against the road side of the wall.
- A shallow dry ditch along the foot of the wall.
- The stony road, with rubbed stones and wheel ruts, running east-west.
- The road's southern edge: a bare, ragged lip of loose stones.
- Below the lip, the ground falls steeply away south: burnt scree with grey thorn clumps, down to a dense belt of grey thorn scrub with a sheltered hollow of dust inside it, then a dry stony wadi far below and burnt hills beyond in the haze.

THE LIGHT AND THE LOOK (every image)
One hard white sun stands high in the south. Shadows are short and hard and fall north. Real deep shade fills a third or more of each frame: tree crowns, wall joints, thorn interiors, stone undersides. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
