The same gate leaf as the reference image, at the same sunset and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the rope lashing where it binds two weathered timber poles of the uphill leaf. Flattened plant-fibre strands, frayed ends, a dust-polished splice and a split in the silver-grey wood run across the frame, with grit packed along the fibres and an old crack following the grain. Rounded wear shines on the wood where the rope has rubbed it, and pale dust sits in the splice. The same low sunset sun grazes the raised rope and the wood from the source side. The crevices, the split and the undersides of the strands fall dark, and that shadow covers a third of the frame and more. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
