The same ledge as the reference image, at the same sunset and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the upper lip of the natural limestone bedrock. Chalk-pale stone, shallow polished hollows, fine pits and hairline cracks hold settled earth, two small barley crumbs and one fallen olive leaf. A thin line of grit follows the hollow where the top has worn smooth. The same low sunset sun grazes the raised stone from the source side. The pits, the cracks and the near face of the lip fall dark, and that shadow covers a third of the frame and more. Every grain of the stone stays sharp under fine film grain. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
