The same farm gate as the reference image, the same posts, lintel, olive-wood leaf, bench, trough and stand on the road, now at first light. The camera still stands eight metres outside the east gateway, facing west. Grey light lies over the eastern hills behind the camera, and the view is lit from the front. Worn earth fills the lower foreground, the road one metre to the LEFT. The whole gateway sits eight metres ahead at centre: the south post and the open leaf on the LEFT, the north post on the RIGHT, the clay lamp burning its last in the arched niche. The stone bench is on the RIGHT and the stone trough on the LEFT. The east wall runs north on the RIGHT. Past the trough on the LEFT the wall stands in front of the cistern inside the yard. Through the opening the hoof-marked lane runs to the open doorway of Eliab's house at centre, about forty-two metres ahead. The last flame is a short warm point in the niche on the RIGHT. The broad grey light from behind picks out the road-facing stone, and shadows stretch west into the yard. The yard, the wall's west courses and the doorway interior hold deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
