The same square as the reference image, at the same dusk and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the paving at the inner mouth of the west gate, looking across the square. The last sun through the gate lies at the CENTRE, one metre ahead, a pale band on the near stones, the light coming from behind the camera so the view is lit from the front. The cook-fire is on the LEFT at nine metres, its smoke pulled west, back toward the gate, by the hot east wind. Houses and shops stand on the RIGHT at nine metres, the square's paving beside them. Kneeling camels and roped bales are at the CENTRE, eleven metres ahead, still on the flags. The street up into the city runs on the LEFT at twenty-three metres, and the street lamps, coming on one by one, show on the LEFT at twenty-nine, small flames with a short reach. Beyond the sun-shaft the shops and the far camels fall into the wall's shadow, deep shadow over more than a third of the frame. Grit from the east wind lies in the joints of the paving. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
