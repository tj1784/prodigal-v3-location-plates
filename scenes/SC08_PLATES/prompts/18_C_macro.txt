The same gate as the reference image, at the same rain-dark dusk and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the rain-darkened limestone sill of the arched lamp niche, every soaked pit and old oil stain sharp. The clay lamp's trimmed flame sits at the upper edge, bent by the wind off the western hills, and moths cling near it.
