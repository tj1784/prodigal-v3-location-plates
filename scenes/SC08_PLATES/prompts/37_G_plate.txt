The same farm as the reference image. The hour is night, and the camera has moved from the gate to inside the fold, facing the barred south gate. The fold fills the CENTRE from two metres. A clay lamp burns on the fold gatepost at the CENTRE, two metres ahead, and the view is backlit by that flame: the post and the barred gate are dark shapes against it. A young black goat stands at the CENTRE, about a metre and a half off, head lowered against its mother, and the she-goat stands ahead on the RIGHT, close, still. Beyond the lamp's short reach the cistern is on the LEFT at seventeen metres, the yard on the LEFT at twenty-one, the eaves and the ovens on the RIGHT, and the threshing floor at the CENTRE near thirty-two, with the terrace edge and a tree farther out, all lost in the dark. Far below, past the gate, a few village fires show as tiny points, about six hundred metres down the hill, too far to light the fold. Moths circle the near flame. Deep shadow covers the ground past the animals and more than two-thirds of the frame. The gate lamp across the yard stays outside this view. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
