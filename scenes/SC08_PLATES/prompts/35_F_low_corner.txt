The same gate as the reference image, at the same harvest afternoon and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the ground in the wall shade beside the trough. The trough's stone lip is on the RIGHT at one metre, and the wall shade covers the RIGHT and the CENTRE. A camel stands on the LEFT, close, head lowered toward the water. A donkey stands ahead on the LEFT, about five metres off, sheaves roped on its back. The floor track is on the LEFT at seven metres, the cistern on the RIGHT at nine, and the threshing floor and the terrace edge farther on. The sun is ahead on the right, so this low ground is inside the wall's shade: deep shadow covers more than a third of the frame, and a thin warm edge catches the far lip of the trough. Dust and a little chaff from the floor settle in the shade. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
