The same fold as the reference image, at the same night and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the straw just inside the barred gate, looking north into the fold. The clay lamp on the gatepost is behind the camera, about one metre off, so the near straw is lit from the front. A young black goat stands on the RIGHT, close, head lowered against its mother, and the she-goat stands ahead, still. The bedded flock lies farther ahead on the LEFT, about six metres off, past the lamp's reach and almost lost in the dark. The fold opens on the RIGHT at five metres, and the calf pen and the ox byre are on the RIGHT at eight. The yard and the yard wall are at the CENTRE at ten metres. Deep shadow covers everything beyond the lamp's short pool, more than two-thirds of the low frame. Moths move in the light behind the lens. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
