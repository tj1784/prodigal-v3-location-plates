The same threshing floor as the reference image, at the same harvest afternoon and in the same light, with the camera at the same south-west rim turned a quarter to the left. Stacked sheaves of bound wheat stand on the RIGHT, two metres off, at the floor's edge. The yard opens on the RIGHT at fourteen metres. Ahead at the CENTRE the ovens sit at twenty-four metres, the eaves at twenty-eight, and the small house at twenty-nine, its open east doorway a dark rectangle at thirty-two metres, the room inside in deep shade. The fold and its barred gate are on the RIGHT at thirty-three metres, the yard wall at the CENTRE at thirty-five, the calf pen on the RIGHT, and the ox byre farther right. One tree stands on the LEFT at thirty-six metres, and another at the CENTRE near fifty metres. The sun is to the left: it lights the left faces of the sheaves, and their right sides, the doorway and the ground under the eaves fall into deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. Chaff from the wheat hangs in the left-hand sun. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
