The same square as the reference image, at the same dusk and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: a limestone flag of the square, hoof-scored and scratched by rope, with grit from the hot east wind lying in the scores. The last sun is ahead and the stone is backlit, so the near half, under the shadow of the shops, is deep dark, and a thin warm line catches the far edge. A few fibres of bast rope sit in one score, and the hoof marks overlap, older cuts under the fresh hoof marks. The camels, the cook-fire and the west gate fall outside this close framing. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
