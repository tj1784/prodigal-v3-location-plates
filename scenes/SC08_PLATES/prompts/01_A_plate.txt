The same threshing floor as the reference image. The hour is now harvest afternoon, and the camera stands on open ground at the south-west rim, facing across the wheat. Loose wheat lies spread over the circular stone, two metres ahead at the CENTRE, and the worn nine-metre floor itself sits six metres ahead at the CENTRE. The floor track runs on at the CENTRE, ten metres off. On the RIGHT the village footpath and the low terrace edge sit at twelve and thirteen metres. On the LEFT the packed yard opens at fourteen metres, and farther off, small in the haze, the cistern, the trough, the one gate, the fold and its barred gate, and the road east. One hazy sun from the south-west stands behind the camera, so the view is lit from the front: the near wheat is warm, and the stems throw their shadows forward across the stone. Chaff from the wheat on the floor hangs in that sun. The terrace edge on the right lays a long dark band, and the folds of the wheat hold deep shadow over more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
