The same threshing floor as the reference image, at the same harvest afternoon and in the same light, with the camera at the same south-west rim turned a quarter to the right. The low terrace edge runs away on the RIGHT, four metres off, its rough fieldstone courses dropping with the slope. On the LEFT the village footpath slants down at fifteen metres, and far on the LEFT, about eighty metres off, the road east continues toward the valley. The wheat and the circular floor lie behind the camera. One hazy sun stands to the right: it strikes the outer face of the terrace edge and leaves the path and the near slope in deep shadow, a dark third of the frame and more. A little chaff from the floor behind drifts across the lit stone. The far road sits pale in the warm-grey haze. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
