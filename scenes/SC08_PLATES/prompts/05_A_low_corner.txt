The same threshing floor as the reference image, at the same harvest afternoon and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the ground at the south-west rim, looking along the stone into the wheat. Loose wheat fills the CENTRE from one metre, and the worn circular floor runs on at the CENTRE, five metres ahead. The floor track continues at the CENTRE near nine metres. On the RIGHT the village footpath and the terrace edge sit close above the straw. On the LEFT the yard, the cistern, the trough and the distant gate remain small beyond the rim. The hazy sun stands behind the camera, so the view is lit from the front: the near straw is warm, and the hollows under the stems are deep shadow, covering more than a third of the low frame. Chaff from the wheat on the floor drifts just above the lens. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
