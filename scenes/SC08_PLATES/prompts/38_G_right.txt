The same fold as the reference image, at the same night and in the same light, with the camera inside the fold turned a quarter to the right. The fold lies on the LEFT at three metres, and the barred gate on the LEFT at four. The yard is on the RIGHT at nine metres, the yard wall on the RIGHT at ten. On the LEFT the eaves, the small house, its dark east doorway and the ovens gather from nineteen to twenty-two metres, the room inside the doorway lost in shade. Trees stand at the CENTRE and on the LEFT farther off, and the terrace edge shows far left. The clay lamp on the fold gatepost is to the LEFT, two metres off, a small flame lighting only the near post and a patch of wall. Past that glow the house and the yard are deep night, shadow over more than two-thirds of the frame. The bedded flock and the young goat remain behind this turn. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
