The same square as the reference image, at the same dusk and in the same light, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the left. Houses and shops line the LEFT at five metres, and the square's paving runs on the LEFT beside them. On the RIGHT the cook-fire burns at thirteen metres, its smoke pulled west by the hot east wind, and the kneeling camels with their roped bales stand at fourteen metres, still, heads down, the ropes tight over the bales. A building face closes the RIGHT at twenty-two metres. The sun is ahead on the right, level and dusty. The shop wall on the left and the ground under the camels lie in the wall's shadow, deep shadow over more than a third of the frame, while the fire and the last sun catch the smoke and the near edges of the bales. Grit from the east wind hangs over the paving. The street lamps are behind this turn. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
