The same threshing floor as the reference image, at the same harvest afternoon and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the trodden limestone rim of the circular floor, every pit and straw-scratch sharp, with loose wheat stems and chaff from the threshing caught in the cracks. The hazy sun from behind grazes the grain. The near half of the stone lies in the shadow of the stems across it, a deep dark over half the frame, while the far lip takes a thin warm line. Straw-scratches cross the pits, and a few broken grains sit in one crack. The yard, the gate and the terrace fall outside this close framing. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
