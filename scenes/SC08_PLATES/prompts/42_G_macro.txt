The same fold as the reference image, at the same night and in the same light, as one worn surface filling the whole frame: the soot-darkened clay rim of the lamp on the fold gatepost, and the rubbed wood of the post under it, every pit, oil stain and raised fibre sharp. The flame at the rim is the light, backlighting the lip, so the near half of the clay lies in deep shadow and a thin warm edge catches the far curve. Soot cakes the inner curve of the clay rim, and a few pale moths blur at the flame. The fold, the goats and the gate fall outside this close framing. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
