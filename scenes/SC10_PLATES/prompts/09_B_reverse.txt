The same pig fields as the reference image, at the same cold hour before dawn, with the camera at the same spot turned half round toward the pen. The pig pen's low wall of piled field stones stands six metres off on the right, dark muck inside, and dark bristly pigs cluster there, one group about ten metres off on the right and one about fourteen metres off on the right. In the centre, six metres off, the fire is down to embers in a ring of blackened stones, a thin smoke. The citizen's booth stands eight metres off in the centre, rough poles and a reed-mat roof on stone footings. The pen gate is nine metres off on the right, its wooden leaf hung on the west post and swung open outward. The cart track from the estate lies twelve metres off in the centre. The bare field opens thirty-two metres off on the left, and the low stone wall lies thirty-six metres off on the right. The grey dawn is behind the camera, so the view is lit from the front, and the embers ahead warm the booth. Deep shadow fills the pen and the ground under the reed roof, more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
