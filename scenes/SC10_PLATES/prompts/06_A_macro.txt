The same estate as the reference image, at the same hard pale morning, as one close-up texture filling the whole frame: the sun-faded mud plaster of the estate wall where the morning shade meets the light. Straw and grit are locked in the clay, fine cracks run through the ochre surface, a corner is chipped, and dust sits in every hollow. The morning sun ahead on the left rakes the plaster at a grazing angle, so the raised grains go pale and the cracks fall into deep shadow. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
