The same estate as the reference image, at the same hard pale morning, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the left. The road along the wall lies four metres off on the left, and the track toward the pig fields lies eleven metres off on the left, both pale grit running out into the haze. On the right, three metres off, the strip of morning shade darkens the dust. The dry plain sits on the right at six metres, and the estate court shows on the right at eight metres past the mud-brick wall, the same packed earth. Fifteen metres on the right the storerooms and byres stand, the same long low mud-brick range, flat earthen roof, small high slits, sun-faded ochre plaster and weathered timber lintels. The sun is ahead on the right, so that side of the view is backlit: the byres' right edges burn pale, their faces fall dark, and deep shadow covers the right third while grit crosses the hard light. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
