The same estate as the reference image, at the same hard pale morning and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the ground in the morning shade, looking west along the foot of the wall. The foreground is trodden dust, cracked, scattered with grit and a few dry stalks, all held in the deep shade. On the right the base of the mud-brick wall rises at the frame edge, straw flecks locked in the clay, the plaster worn back to the brick. Beyond it the estate court lies low and dark on the right, and the storerooms and the citizen's house sit small on the right, about seven and sixteen metres off, their flat roofs against the haze. The dry plain opens on the right at three metres where the shade thins. The one light is the morning sun ahead on the left: a hard pale band crosses the left distance, and deep shadow covers more than a third of the frame along the ground. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
