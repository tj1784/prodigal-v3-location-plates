The same pig fields as the reference image, at the same dusk, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the right. The lone carob's low wide crown stands seven metres off on the left, the same thick dark trunk and dusty leaves. The bare field of rooted dust fills the centre and runs about thirty-one metres off, stark and hoof-marked, pale in the last light. The sun is to the right, low, so the carob's right side is warm and its left side falls into deep shadow, and that shadow covers the left third of the frame. The citizen's new fire lies off to the right, ten metres away, a small bright flame and a thread of smoke in the same quarter as the sun, warming the near dust. Long shadows stretch toward the left across the open ground. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
