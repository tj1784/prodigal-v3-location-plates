The same place as the reference image, on the same night and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the ground beside the fire, looking toward the booth. Ash, blackened stones and trodden dust fill the foreground, warm on the fire's side. The citizen's fire burns one metre ahead on the right, the one warm light, moths in the flame. The booth's rough poles and reed mat rise two metres off in the centre, the near face flared, the far side in deep shadow. The pen's low stone wall lies twelve metres off on the right, and the bare field opens twenty-eight metres off on the left into the night. Deep shadow covers the left of the frame and the ground beyond the fire, more than a third, under starlight and a pale dusty haze. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
