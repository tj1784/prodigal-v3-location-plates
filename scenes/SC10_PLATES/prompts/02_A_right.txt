The same estate as the reference image, at the same hard pale morning, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the right toward the gate. The strip of morning shade lies one metre off on the left, the dry plain lies on the left at three metres, and the estate court opens on the left at four metres, packed earth inside the wall. In the centre, eight metres off, the estate gate breaks the wall: a wide opening between square mud-brick posts, a weathered wooden leaf hung on the west post and swung open into the court. The estate wall, mud brick about two and a half metres high, stands in the centre at eleven metres. The citizen's house shows on the left at eleven metres, the same flat-roofed range. On the right, five metres off, a narrow track leaves north-east toward the pig fields, and at eight metres the road along the wall runs toward the city, both pale grit lost in the haze. The sun is behind on the left. It lights the left faces of the posts and the wall, and long shadows stretch to the right across the road, with deep shadow holding the lower third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
