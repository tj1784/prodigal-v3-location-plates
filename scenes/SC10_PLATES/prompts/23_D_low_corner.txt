The same pig fields as the reference image, at the same dusk and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the rooted dust, looking north across the field with the herd. Hoof-marked dust and long shadows fill the foreground. A dark bristly pig stands about ten metres off on the left, another about seventeen metres off on the right, and the herd runs through the centre toward the thorn scrub sixty-eight metres off on the right. The low stone wall lies on the left at about thirty-nine metres. The low sun is behind the camera, so the view is lit from the front and the shadows stretch away from the lens, covering more than a third of the ground. The new fire behind the camera warms the near dust with a small flame. Thorn trees stand in the centre and on the right at the far scrub, grey against the pale haze. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
