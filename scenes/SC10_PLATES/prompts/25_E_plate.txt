The same pig fields as the reference image, now at night instead of noon, the camera moved from beside the carob out into the east thorn scrub, facing through the thorns, the pen and the troughs lying back across the dust. The bare field, the low stone walls, the timber troughs, the reed booth and the lone carob stay as the reference shows them. The bare field and the thorn scrub both begin five metres off on the right. In the centre, nine metres off, the thorns where the pig is caught rise in hooked grey branches, and a dark bristly pig is fast in them, about eight metres off in the centre, legs tangled in the spines. Other thorn trees stand from about eight to twenty-five metres, on the left, in the centre and on the right, dense and grey. The one warm light is the citizen's fire behind the camera, seventy-eight metres off, so a faint warm light falls on the near thorns from the front. Starlight and a pale dusty haze sit over the rest. Deep night shadow covers far more than a third of the frame, the scrub almost black beyond the fire's reach. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
