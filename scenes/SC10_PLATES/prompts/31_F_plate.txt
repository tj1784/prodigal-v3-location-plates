The same pig fields as the reference image, now at night instead of before dawn, the camera moved from just south of the troughs to a stand beside the booth at the pen, facing the shelter. The bare field of rooted dust, the timber troughs, the low stone pen, the reed booth, the lone carob and the fire ring stay where the reference places them. The citizen's booth stands in the centre, two metres off, rough poles and a reed-mat roof on stone footings. The pig pen's low wall of piled field stones stands twelve metres off on the right, dark muck inside. The bare field of rooted dust opens twenty-eight metres off on the left. The low field wall of piled stones lies thirty-one metres off on the right. The citizen's fire burns one metre ahead on the right, the one warm light: it flares the near side of the booth, moths move in the flame because of the fire, and the left of the frame falls into deep night shadow, more than a third. Starlight and a pale dusty haze hold the sky over the black field. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
