The same pig fields as the reference image, at the same hard pale noon, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the right. The noon shade reaches two metres off on the right, the hard-edged dark pool under the carob cutting across the near dust. The citizen's booth stands fourteen metres off on the right, the same low shelter of rough poles and a reed-mat roof on stone footings, small against the open field. Around it the bare rooted dust runs away, hoof-marked and stark, with the low horizon and the pale warm-grey haze. The sun is ahead on the left, high and hard, so the booth's left edge is bright and its reed roof throws a deep shadow across the right foreground. That shadow covers more than a third of the frame. Grit moves in the dry wind across the lit dust. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
