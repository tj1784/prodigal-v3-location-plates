The same scrub as the reference image, on the same night and in the same light, with the lens a hand's breadth above the ground among the thorn litter, looking toward the caught pig. Dry spines, pale dust and broken twigs fill the foreground. In the centre, about eight metres off, the dark bristly pig is fast in the hooked thorns, the branches rising over the lens. The bare field and the scrub open five metres off on the right, with more thorn trees on the left, in the centre and on the right. The citizen's fire, seventy-eight metres behind the camera, lays a faint warm grazed light on the nearest spines. Beyond that the scrub is deep night shadow, more than a third of the frame, under starlight and a pale dusty haze. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
