On the left, five metres off, the narrower track forks north-east toward the pig fields, the fields themselves far off inside the haze. The dry plain fills the right of the distance at thirty-seven metres and beyond: cracked pale earth, thin clumps of dead grass, grit sliding in the dry wind. The wall and the gate remain behind the camera. The sun is behind on the right, so the near dust is lit from behind, long shadows run forward and toward the left, and deep shadow covers the foreground third while the far plain sits pale under a warm-grey sky. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
