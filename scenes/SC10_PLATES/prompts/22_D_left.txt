The same pig fields as the reference image, at the same dusk, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the left. The three long timber troughs stand two metres off on the right, close, hollowed and dark with dry curled carob husks, small flies over the pods. The pig pen's low stone wall stands eleven metres off on the left. The bare field of rooted dust fills the centre, and the low wall of piled field stones runs in the centre at thirty-four metres, uneven and unmortared. The low sun is to the left, so it rakes across the trough rims and throws their long shadows to the right. The citizen's new fire lies to the left, ten metres off, a small warm flame on that same side. Deep shadow covers the right faces of the troughs and the lower third of the frame. Dust drifts in the low light. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
