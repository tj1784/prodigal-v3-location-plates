The same pig fields as the reference image, now at dusk instead of before dawn, the camera moved from just south of the troughs to a stand by the troughs a little farther east, still looking north. The three timber troughs, the pen, the reed booth and the lone carob stay where the reference places them. The bare field of rooted dust opens thirty-eight metres off on the left, and the low wall of piled field stones runs on the left at thirty-nine metres. Thorn scrub marks the east boundary sixty-eight metres off on the right, with thorn trees from about seventy-two to seventy-nine metres in the centre and on the right. Dark bristly pigs spread on the dust: one about ten metres off on the left, one about seventeen metres off on the right, and the herd through the centre from about twenty-nine to forty-five metres. One pig has drifted ahead, about forty-nine metres off in the centre, toward the scrub on the right. The low sun is behind the camera, so the view is lit from the front and long shadows stretch away over the dust. The citizen's new fire, ten metres behind the camera, warms the near ground. Deep cast shadow covers a wide third of the field. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
