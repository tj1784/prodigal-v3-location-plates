The same place as the reference image, on the same night, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the left. The lone carob stands fourteen metres off on the left, the same thick dark trunk and low dusty crown. The bare field of rooted dust opens twenty-eight metres off on the right, stark even in the dark. Thorn scrub lies eighty metres off on the left, and thorn trees run from about eighty-four to a hundred and fifteen metres, all on the left, a grey band in the haze. The citizen's fire is behind the camera, one metre off, so the view is lit from the front by that one warm light: the near dust and the carob's near side take the flame, and the far field on the right falls into deep night shadow, more than a third of the frame. Starlight and a pale dusty haze hold the sky. A few moths cross the warm air above the lens. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
