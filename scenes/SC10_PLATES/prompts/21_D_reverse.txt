The same pig fields as the reference image, at the same dusk, with the camera at the same spot turned half round toward the pen and the fire. The pig pen's low stone wall stands nine metres off on the right, dark muck inside. The citizen's new fire burns ten metres off in the centre, a small flame in a ring of blackened stones, smoke rising off the wood. The booth stands twelve metres off in the centre, the same rough poles and reed-mat roof. The pen gate is thirteen metres off on the right, the wooden leaf hung on the west post and swung open. The bare field opens thirty-five metres off on the left, and the low wall of piled field stones crosses the centre at forty metres. The low sun is ahead, so the view is backlit, and the fire ahead backlights the booth and the pen as well, their edges warm against the pale sky. Deep shadow fills the pen, the ground under the reed roof and the foreground third. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
