Dark bristly pigs stand in that pen, about eighty-three metres off on the right, small against the fire. The fire is ahead, so the view is backlit by that one warm light, a small flame at the end of the field, the pen wall edged with it. Starlight fills the rest. Deep shadow covers the left of the frame and almost all of the ground. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
