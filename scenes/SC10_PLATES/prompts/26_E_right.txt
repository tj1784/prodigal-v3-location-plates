The same scrub as the reference image, on the same night and in the same starlight, with the camera at the same spot turned a quarter to the right. The bare field and the thorn scrub lie five metres off on the left. Thorn trees stand thick on the left and in the centre from about eight to twenty-five metres, hooked grey branches, and one thorn stands about twenty-three metres off on the right. The citizen's fire is behind on the right, seventy-eight metres off, the one warm light, so a faint warm edge touches the right-hand branches from behind and the rest of the scrub falls into deep night shadow, more than a third of the frame. Dusty pale haze and a few stars show through the crowns. The ground is dry litter, spines and pale dust. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
