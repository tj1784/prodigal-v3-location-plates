The same fold gate as the reference image, just after sunrise, as a close texture of its olive-wood bar. The lens comes within a hand's breadth of the bar, which fills the centre of the frame. The wood is polished pale along the middle from long use, and dark in the old cracks. Caught cream and brown wool fibres and fine straw dust sit in the grain. The fold's straw lies just outside the close frame on the LEFT, about 1 metre away. The calf rail and the ox byre sit 10 metres away on the RIGHT, beyond the bar. The sun lies to the RIGHT, the only light, grazing the raised grain and the fibres from the east. The bar's underside and the dust-filled cracks cover over a third of the texture in deep contact shadow. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
