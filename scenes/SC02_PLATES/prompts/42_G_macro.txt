The same well coping as the reference image, at night, as a close texture of its fire-facing upper lip. The lens comes within a hand's breadth of the stone, and a deep old rope groove, a smooth chalky ridge, dust-filled pits and the damp inner edge fill the frame. The coping's near shoulder lies 1 metre away on the LEFT. The brushwood fire is behind on the RIGHT, 2 metres off, the only local light, grazing the ridge and leaving the groove in deep shadow. That shadow covers over a third of the texture. The far hill lamp also lies behind on the RIGHT, outside this close frame. Open earth continues 7 metres away on the RIGHT, and a house front and the village-edge wall sit about 9 metres off. The soot on the fire-facing stone belongs to that same brushwood flame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
