The same room as the reference image, at grey dawn, as a close texture of the reed mat beneath the west-wall hand mill. The lens comes within a hand's breadth of the frayed weave, the worn millstone edge crossing the upper frame at the centre. Interlaced reed fibres, crushed pale flour and small patches of compacted earth fill the rest. Flour sits in the grooves left by grinding, and the handled edge of the reed is darker and polished. The east doorway lies outside this close frame, ahead on the RIGHT. Grey dawn from that doorway is the only light, ahead on the RIGHT, grazing the raised fibres and the pits in the stone. Woven hollows and the contact shadow under the millstone cover over a third of the texture. The flour belongs to the mill's working surface. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
