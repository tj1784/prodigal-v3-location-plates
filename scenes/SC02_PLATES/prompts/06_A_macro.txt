The same room as the reference image, before dawn, as a close texture of the reed mat beneath the west-wall hand mill. From the mat's south-west edge, looking north-east, the lens comes within a hand's breadth of its frayed weave. The worn millstone edge enters across the upper LEFT; interlaced reed fibres, crushed flour and small patches of compacted earth fill the rest of the frame. Pale flour settles into the grooves left by repeated grinding, with darker polished reed along the mat's handled edge. The east doorway lies outside this close frame ahead on the RIGHT, roughly four metres away; its threshold lamp supplies the only light. That small flame grazes the raised fibres and stone pits, leaving over a third of the texture in deep woven and contact shadow. The flour belongs to the mill's working surface. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
