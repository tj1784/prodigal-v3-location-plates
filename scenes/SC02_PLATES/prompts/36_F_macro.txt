The same lantern ledge as the reference image, deep at night, as a close texture of the chipped limestone immediately beside the flame. The lens comes within a hand's breadth of the stone, and the ledge fills the frame: chalky pits, fine dust, a dry thorn splinter and old hairline cracks. The pierced clay casing of the lantern sits at the centre edge, its oil flame ahead, backlighting the near grains of the stone and lighting only this surface. The village fire lies ahead on the LEFT, 132 metres off, outside this close frame. The road, the village roofs and the dark pasture stay beyond the stone. Contact shadow in the pits and cracks covers over a third of the texture. The dust belongs to the worn ledge. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
