The same house table as the reference image, late at night, as a close texture of its olive-wood edge beside the one burning lamp. Pale rubbed grain, old shallow cuts, a few bread crumbs and the base of a clay cup fill the frame. The lamp's wick grazes the wood from just beside the centre, and the table top lies 1 metre ahead at the centre. The east doorway and the water jar sit 2 metres away on the RIGHT, outside this close view. The oil jars and the storage jars lie 2 to 3 metres away on the LEFT, and the hearth ring lies 3 metres ahead at the centre. The gate lamp is to the RIGHT, 36 metres off in the yard, a small amber beyond the door. Contact shadow under the cup and in the cuts covers over a third of the texture. The crumbs belong to the bread on this table. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
