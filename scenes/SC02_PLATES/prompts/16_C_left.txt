The same room as the reference image, at grey dawn, the camera above the table turned a quarter left to face west. The west wall stands 1.5 metres ahead. Two smaller clay oil jars occupy the RIGHT, about 2 metres away. The stone hand mill sits about 2 metres ahead on the LEFT, on its frayed reed mat, pale flour caught in the weave. The shoulder of a tall sealed storage jar enters at the far RIGHT, and the jars continue north along this wall. Two wooden stools stand between the table and the wall. Worn lime plaster gives way to mud brick at the floor, and old soot darkens the beams. Grey dawn lies behind the camera, the only light, falling on the jar shoulders and the mill from the front. Dense contact shadows sit under the stools, the mill and the jar bases, and with the roof darkness they cover over a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
