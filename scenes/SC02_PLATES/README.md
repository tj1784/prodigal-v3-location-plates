## The locked design

- **Eliab's room.** Seven paces by four, 5.25 m north to south and 3 m east to west. Facing the hearth from the middle: front, the fire-blackened hearth ring, the clay pot and the arched lamp niche; right, the timber doorway onto the yard and the water jar; behind, the mud-brick sleeping bench with sheepskins and blankets, the reed mat and the sling on its peg; left, the sealed storage jars, the oil jars and the hand mill on its flour-dusted reed mat; middle, the low heavy olive-wood table and the stools. Before dawn the only light is the clay lamp at the outer threshold. At dawn that lamp and the others rest with dark wicks, and grey dawn through the doorway is the only light.
- **The farm.** A walled yard about 34 m by 30 m, dry-stone fieldstone about 1.7 m high. One east gateway, 2.7 m clear, between square posts under a timber lintel. The olive-wood leaf hinges on the south post and folds into the yard. The arched niche is in the north post. A stone bench sits outside north of the gate; a stone trough sits outside south of it. The fold is chest-high on the north side, with a barred timber gate. The ox byre and the railed calf pen stand east of the fold. The house occupies the west wall. Two clay ovens stand south-east of the house. The nine-metre threshing floor lies south of the yard, two metres below the gate, with a bread basket, a flail and barley sheaves at the before-dawn hour. An old fig stands outside the east wall.
- **The hours on the farm.** Just after sunrise the fold holds a hundred small earth-coloured sheep, one with a torn ear, and a lean brown dog on the near wall. At dusk ninety-nine rest in the fold; dust from their return hangs in the gateway, and the low sun is the light, the lamp still in the niche. Late at night the hundred are back, thorn twigs in the torn-ear fleece, the niche lamp burns again, the doorway spills amber, and the dog rests under the bench.
- **The valley.** One pierced clay lantern on the search track, 777 m ahead at the centre, and the village fire at the well, 699 m ahead at the centre. The farm terrace is the dark roof 79 m on the right. The thorn gully is 791 m on the left of the lantern. The hill pasture is empty at this hour. The flock is in the fold.
- **The well ground.** A low circular coping with a timber beam, a brushwood fire about 8 m ahead at the centre, three resting stones around it, houses to the west and south, and a low wall with the black slope beyond. Sparks and smoke come off the brushwood. The far lamp is the minute point ahead at the centre.

## The look

Bible §4: one source at the real hour, real deep shadow over a third of the frame, a muted sun-faded earth palette, a pale warm-grey haze, fine grain. Each prompt ends on that line. Each macro ends on "Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain." At night the lantern, the brushwood fire or the niche lamp is the flame, and the rest of the frame stays dark.
