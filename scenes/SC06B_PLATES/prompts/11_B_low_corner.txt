The same morning bend, the lens a hand's breadth above the ground at the roadside, about twelve centimetres off the dust. The road lies four metres away on the LEFT. The last spur of the hill rises six metres away on the LEFT, pale rock and thin grass. The boulders at its foot stand six metres ahead at the centre, chipped faces large against the sky. Dry grass clumps between the stones. Fine dust sits in the ruts. One low morning sun stands ahead on the RIGHT and catches the boulder tops and the spur's right edge. Shadows from the boulders fall toward the left and toward the lens, and the dark undersides cover more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
