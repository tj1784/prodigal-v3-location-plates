The same dawn gateway, turned a quarter to the left and now facing south. The camera stays at (8, -1.8). The road crosses the foreground at the centre, a metre away, and the foot of the hill track lies two metres away on the LEFT. The floor track leaves the road two metres away on the RIGHT and descends beside the outer yard wall. The yard shows eleven metres away on the RIGHT, past that wall. The village footpath and the terrace edge cross the slope about thirty-one metres ahead near the centre. The threshing floor, hard worked earth inside low set stones, lies thirty-three metres away on the RIGHT, about two metres below the gate. The path down to the far field continues beyond it on the right. One low dawn sun stands to the LEFT and grazes the track stones and the terrace edge. The right-hand faces of the wall and the floor's rim fall into deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
