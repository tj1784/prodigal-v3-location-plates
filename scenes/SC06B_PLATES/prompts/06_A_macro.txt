The same dawn gateway, closed in until one worn surface fills the frame. The lens stands at (0.95, 0.05), facing west, half a metre from the road-facing east face of the north fieldstone gatepost. Pale limestone fills the picture: rounded arrises, chalky pits, old lime caught in the grain, and hairline cracks packed with road dust. Grazing dawn light comes from behind the camera and picks the raised grains. The pits and the lower joints stay in deep shadow. The arched niche just enters the top of the post, its lip rubbed smooth at shoulder height. Just outside the crop, the first light on the yard lane lies a metre away on the LEFT, and the yard wall continues a metre away on the RIGHT. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
