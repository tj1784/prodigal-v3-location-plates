The same dawn gateway, turned a quarter to the right and now facing north. The camera stays on the open road at (8, -1.8). Dry grass and loose limestone fill the near ground. The hill track to the pasture begins five metres away on the RIGHT and climbs. An old fig stands ten metres ahead near the centre, twisted trunk and low grey crown whole in the frame. The east yard wall runs on the LEFT about ten metres off, the yard behind it. The calf pen and the ox byre show beyond the wall on the LEFT, and the chest-high fold, with the farm flock on the straw, lies further left at about twenty-four metres. The same rough fieldstone, olive timber and packed earth remain. One low dawn sun stands to the RIGHT and rakes the fig and the track. The left faces of the trunk, the wall and the byre fall into deep shadow, more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
