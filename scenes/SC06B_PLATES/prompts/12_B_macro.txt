The same morning bend, closed in until one worn surface fills the frame. The lens stands at (1038.7, -576.75), facing west, about a metre from the east face of the boulders at the spur's foot. Pale limestone fills the picture: chipped ridges, chalky pits, dust packed into old cracks, and a thorn-scratched grain. Dawn light comes from behind the camera and grazes the raised chips. The pits stay in deep shadow. A pale scuff crosses the upper ridge, and fine grit sits in every pit. That east face is the whole frame. Outside the crop the spur continues at the centre, and the road lies three metres away on the LEFT. Muted sun-faded earth colours, fine film grain.
