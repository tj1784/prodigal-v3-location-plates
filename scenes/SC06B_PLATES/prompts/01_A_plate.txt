The same farm gateway as the reference image, now at dawn instead of dusk, from the same road stand. The camera is eight metres outside Eliab's east gateway in Judea, first century AD, at (8, -1.8), facing west. Worn road fills the foreground, its near edge a metre away on the LEFT. Eight metres ahead at the centre is a 2.7-metre opening between 0.9-metre fieldstone posts, a timber lintel and a dry-stone wall 1.7 metres high. The north post is on the RIGHT, a cold clay lamp in its niche, the stone bench beside it. The south post is on the LEFT, the olive-wood leaf open into the yard, the trough on the LEFT. The farm dog lies just right of centre at the north post. The calf pen, the ox byre and the chest-high fold with the flock lie farther right. The cistern is on the left. Oven smoke rises straight just left of centre. The house doorway lies forty-two metres ahead on the same axis. One low dawn sun stands behind the camera. Outer stone is lit, first light falls through the opening, and deep shadow fills the jambs, the lintel underside and the yard, more than a third of the frame. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
