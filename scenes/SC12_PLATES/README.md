## The locked design

**The well on the road west (A).** A round limestone well at the local origin, about 1.5 m across, and a stone trough at (2.15, 0.15), about 2.0 by 0.6 m. The road west is a pale stony track about 3.6 m wide, running east-west near y = -4.5. A narrow footpath leaves the well toward a hamlet to the north. The ground is burnt brown earth. Morning dust hangs low because of earlier passage. The place is empty.

**The foreign farm (B, C, D, and the slope of E).** One design, from `foreign_farm.json` and the screenplay. A waist-high wall of piled unmortared fieldstone, about 0.9 m thick, runs along y = 1, with a 3.5 m field gap from x = 11 to 14.5. A single tamarisk stands at (0, 0) over the dry ditch. South of the road the land falls as scree and thorn to the scrub and a dry wadi. Inside the wall: cut stubble, a last strip of standing barley, and the spring-fed green fold, the green ground in the dead brown land. The yard is the hens' yard. The long timber table, about 4.8 m, sits at (-6, 35.2) under a vine. The round bread oven is at (12, 37); at harvest morning its smoke rises straight. The low flat-roofed house runs from x = -14 to 9.5 and y = 40 to 47. The pantry is its east room, with a barred timber door on the south wall. The house door is west of that. A small clay figure sits in a wall niche near (-4.85, 40.15), with a short lamp flame before it, under the sun. The stone well is at (-18, 33). The spring is at (-24, 52.2). At noon the earth beside the well is darkened where water has run, and shadows are short. Before dawn the hollow holds dew on thorn, stone and twig, wind in the branches, a few last stars, and the grey line in the east as the one light. The same wall, tamarisk, gap, house, table, oven, niche, well and slope keep these places in every prompt that reaches them.

**The far hills (F).** Another stretch of the road west, its own grid, origin where the road's walker stops. The track is about 3.2 m wide. Dead brown land flanks it. Four small thorn bushes stand on the map. A low line of hills lies far west in the afternoon haze. Heat shimmer and dust come off the dry ground in the afternoon wind.

**The crest of the road home (G).** The valley road of the earlier bend plate, now at dusk. The road east to Jericho climbs to the crest. Ahead: low farm buildings, the thorn gully, the village, the village well, the stone village gate and its elders' bench, the hill pasture with its flock, the hill track, the path to the far field, the far field, and other farms on the next terraces, all at the distances the digest prints. Evening gnats turn in the low sun, and dust lifts off the crest in the evening wind. The sunset is the one light, straight ahead over the hills.
