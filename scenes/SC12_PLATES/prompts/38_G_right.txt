The same crest road as the reference image, at the same dusk, the camera turned a quarter turn right at the same stand, now facing 75 degrees, along the open shoulder. The road east to Jericho lies 2 m away on the LEFT, stony, running out of the left foreground and away along the rise. The hillside beyond the road is open, dry and pale, falling gently, with small stones and a worn verge. The valley, the village and the setting sun's face remain off the left of this turn. One low dusk sun stands to the LEFT, through haze, and grazes the road from that side. Long shadows fall to the right across the verge and the near bank. The ruts and the lee of the bank hold deep shadow across a third of the frame or more. A few evening gnats turn in the low light above the stones, and a little dust moves along the track in the evening wind. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
