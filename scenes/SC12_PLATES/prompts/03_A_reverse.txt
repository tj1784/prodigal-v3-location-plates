The same burnt roadside as the reference image, at the same morning hour and in the same light, the camera turned a half turn at the same stand, now facing 300 degrees, away from the well and into the open land. Burnt brown earth fills the frame from the foreground, its nearest rise 3 m away on the RIGHT, dry, cracked and strewn with small pale stones. The road, the trough, the well and the footpath remain behind the camera. One low morning sun stands ahead on the LEFT, through pale haze, and rakes the ground toward the camera. Long shadows stretch forward from each stone and from every low ridge. The hollows and the camera-facing sides of the stones hold deep shadow across a third of the frame or more. The lit dust stays warm and matte, and a thin veil of road dust hangs in the low light. Muted sun-faded earth colours, a pale warm-grey haze, fine film grain, wide lens, deep focus. The place is empty and still.
